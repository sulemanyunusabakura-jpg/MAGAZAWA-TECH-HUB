Solar Energy Measurement System

Microcontroller-based solar energy monitoring system with display.

Project Overview

The Solar Energy Measurement System is designed to monitor important electrical parameters produced by a solar panel.

A microcontroller receives measurements from voltage and current sensing circuits and presents the information on a display.

Aim of the Project

The aim of this project is to design a microcontroller-based system capable of measuring and displaying solar panel electrical parameters.

Objectives

  • Measure solar panel voltage.
  • Measure solar panel current.
  • Calculate electrical power.
  • Display measurement results.
  • Provide useful information about solar energy production.

Main Components

  • Arduino Uno
  • Solar panel
  • Voltage sensor
  • Current sensor
  • 16×2 LCD display
  • Resistors
  • Connecting wires
  • Power supply

How the System Works

The solar panel produces electrical energy when exposed to sunlight.

The voltage sensing circuit measures the panel voltage while the current sensing circuit measures the current flowing through the load.

The Arduino processes these measurements and sends the results to the display.

Power Calculation

Electrical power can be calculated using:

P = V × I

Where:

  • P = Power in watts (W)
  • V = Voltage in volts (V)
  • I = Current in amperes (A)

For example, if the solar panel produces 12 V and 2 A:

P = 12 × 2 = 24 W

Display Information

The display can show information such as:

  • Solar panel voltage
  • Solar panel current
  • Calculated power
  • System status
